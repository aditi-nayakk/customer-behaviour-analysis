# 📊 Online Retail Customer & Sales Analytics 


## Executive Summary:

Analyzed online retail shopping data to identify customer behavior patterns, top-performing product categories, and revenue drivers. The goal is to translate raw data into actionable insights that support data-driven decisions in marketing strategy, product optimization, and customer engagement.

### Business Problem:

The business lacks clarity on:
- Which product categories perform best across customer segments
- How demographics and purchasing behavior affect revenue
- Whether subscriptions, discounts, and promotions drive repeat purchases

### Methodology:

1. Data Preparation- Cleaned and validated customer and transaction data, filling any missing values.
2. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)- Age groups were created based on consumer life stages and purchasing power to support trend analysis and targeted marketing strategies.
3. SQL & Dashboard Analysis-
   - Used SQL aggregations and window functions to identify top categories per age group.
   - Built an interactive dashboard to visualize KPIs and trends.
   
### Skills:

- Python (Pandas): Data cleaning, feature engineering, EDA
- SQL: CTEs, CASE WHEN, window functions, aggregations
- Data Visualization: Dashboard design & storytelling
- Tools: PostgreSQL, Jupyter Notebook, Python, PowerBI

### Key Findings:

- Clothing was the highest purchase category for each age group both by revenue and volume.
- About 68% of the revenue is generated by Male customers.
- While the average expenditure for subscribers and non-subscribers remained the same, non-subscribers generated 3x more revenue.
- Revenue contribution is fairly evenly distributed among all age groups.

### Next Steps:

- Analyze customer lifetime value (CLV)
- A/B test discount and promo strategies
